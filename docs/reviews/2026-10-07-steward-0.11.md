# Review — steward 0.11

## Why

The reference steward was built against steward 0.9 and 0.10, and in six places it does what the
maintainer ruled rather than what the text says. Its own shapes for `define`'s change kinds and for a
few plan fields live in the shared schemas, marked provisional, where they would grade a second
steward by one build's choices. Two later rulings join them: a fresh steward must accept its first
`define` before any notice channel exists, and the `ask` event must carry what it asks.

**Rulings** (the maintainer), as they bear on the text:
- 2026-10-06: steward 0.11 holds the six departures (PER-e, RUL-d, RUL-f, RUL-a, LEA-c, SCN-b) and
  the shapes taken out of the shared schemas (BRN-b); the rest of the build's proposals go to 0.12,
  and the applier's to applier 0.16.
- The wording of each departure:
  - PER-e: GA-PERSIST-2 covers open plans with an ask, and their answers.
  - RUL-d: a MAY replay of a gap's edge, once, within a bound the implementation states, its
    conditions judged now.
  - RUL-f: MAY coalesce the `occupancy_unknown` notice per rule and room.
  - RUL-a: a lease's end re-evaluates only the rules it refused.
  - LEA-c: MAY end a latchless safety lease after a bound of at least the rule's `ack_within_s`; the
    number is the implementation's.
  - SCN-b: GA-SCN-9 keeps the floor (a visitor never may) and leaves who else may to the steward.
- 2026-10-07, DEF-a: change sets are accepted while `notice_channels` is empty, and refused only when
  they leave a list, once set, without a channel; `define` refuses a channel device that is
  not adopted or offers neither `notify` nor `media.announce`.
- 2026-10-07, the ask's content: the `ask` event carries the asked steps, `steps`, one
  `{ step_id, target, action, args, tier, reason }` per asked step, named as in *Steps*; clients
  detect it by its presence. A plan read was rejected.

The background of each item is in the build's proposals for 0.10, by its id; they are not public, and
the record says what each item is without them.

### Schemas

- `steward/event.ask.json` (new): the `ask` event with `steps`; an example of each kind. It stands
  apart from `steward/event.json`, so nothing validates against it until the harness adopts it.
- The provisional marks are dropped from every `define.<kind>.json`, `define.delete.json`,
  `define.response.json` and `plan.json`, which now cite *Change sets*, *Steps* or *Operations*.
- `define.target.json` gains `account_persons`; its invalid example moves to the valid ones.
- `describe.response.json` requires `persons`; its example carries one.
- `text-map.json`: the ignores for `answer`, `brain_authored` and `persons` are removed, and *Change sets* is mapped, one entry per kind with a schema (all but `home` and `lift_narrowing`) (Q10).
- `event.ask.json`: each `steps` entry is one of the two forms (`oneOf`), and `run_id` is optional; examples for a define's ask and a re-ask.
- `describe.response.json`: optional `edge_replay_s`.
- Types regenerated. **The package `@ludentes/galatea-life-schemas` goes to 0.1.2 (main had taken 0.1.1 for the applier's changes, also pending a publish); its published
  contents changed, so it needs a publish.**

### What the reference steward must change

Left to the build's own plan; this branch changes no build code.
- **DEF-a:** `checkChannels` (in its `define`) refuses every change set while `notice_channels` names
  no `notify` channel, so a fresh steward refuses its first `define`. It must accept change sets while
  the list has never been set, and refuse only one that leaves it, once set, without a `notify`
  channel. Its channel checks (adopted; `notify` or `media.announce`) already match.
- **`ask.steps`:** both places that append an `ask` event (a plan's ask, and a run's re-ask) must
  carry `steps`; the harness's GA-CONF-5 case should check them against the plan's steps and validate
  the event against `steward/event.ask.json`. The owner tool can then show the question from the event.
- **RUL-d:** `describe` must carry `edge_replay_s` (the build replays within 5 minutes, so 300).
- **RUL-f:** the build keeps a rule and room's coalesced notice for the rule's lifetime; it must forget it once the room's occupancy is known again.
- **DEF-a, held notices:** notices raised before any channel is set must be held and delivered once one is; a list of announcing channels alone must be accepted.
- **GA-AUTH-7:** the `ask` event's `steps` must be withheld from a guest or visitor as the plan is.
- **RUL-a:** built only in part (a lifted provisional lease that refused nothing does not count as an
  end); every lease's end must re-evaluate only the rules it refused.
- **Already built, now within the text:** PER-e (plans with no ask lost at a restart), RUL-d (edges
  replayed within 5 minutes), RUL-f (`occupancy_unknown` noticed once per rule and room), LEA-c (a
  latchless safety lease bounded at 15 minutes, above every default `ack_within_s`), SCN-b (members
  and up stop a rule's or a schedule's run; guests and visitors refused).
- **The schemas:** a `target` upsert with `account_persons` now passes the schema; the build, which
  has not built GA-DEF-11 (its PC slice is put off), must refuse or keep it rather than drop it.
- **New negative subjects** need mutations and harness cases: the fourteen above.

### Questions, and the coordinator's rulings (2026-10-07)

The draft raised ten questions; the coordinator ruled on each, and the rows above carry the changes.

1. **A define plan's `steps`:** a `confirm_define` entry is `{ step_id, reason: confirm_define,
   diff }`, the step's own shape; the schema takes either form (`oneOf`). GA-CONF-5.
2. **Who reads the steps:** GA-AUTH-7's floor covers `steps[].args` and a `diff`'s values in
   `events` and `history`, as it covers the plan. GA-AUTH-7, stricter; `asks-with-personal-args`.
3. **A re-ask's `run_id`:** named, optional. GA-CONF-5, moved from the build.
4. **RUL-d's bound:** stated in `describe` as `edge_replay_s`, absent when the steward replays
   none. GA-RULE-1, new field; `replays-past-its-bound`.
5. **RUL-f's span:** while the room's occupancy stays unknown for that rule; the next unknown after
   it was known again notices again. GA-RULE-8; `never-notices-again`; a build change.
6. **LEA-c's `ack_within_s`:** that of the rule's actuation on the leased device, as drafted. No
   change.
7. **DEF-a while no channel is set:** notices are held and delivered when the first channel is set;
   a first `define` setting only a `media.announce` channel is accepted, since a channel is `notify`
   or `media.announce`. The draft had refused it; fixed. GA-DEF-8, GA-NOTE-1;
   `refuses-an-announcing-channel`, `loses-an-early-notice`. **Left for the readers:** with only
   announcing channels, a notice is spoken once and stays held, because GA-NOTE-1 never counts an
   announcement as delivery; the text says so rather than changing GA-NOTE-1.
8. **GA-DEF-12:** "the devices its triggers and conditions name"; selectors in triggers and
   conditions are not part of the shapes. Moved (clarifying).
9. **A `target` upsert** replaces the whole overlay, `account_persons` included, as upsert means
   everywhere; one line in *Change sets*. Clarifying.
10. **The text map:** *Change sets* added to `text-map.json`.
