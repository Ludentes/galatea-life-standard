# Review of the voice standard 0.1, brain 0.4, steward 0.5 and applier 0.10

This record follows the `standard-review` procedure. The commit reviewed is named with pass 1.

## Change record

Written and committed before the texts and before any reader. Directions: **stricter**,
**looser**, **moved**, **new**, **renamed**. The design is
`docs/specs/2026-09-25-voice-architecture-design.md`; the scenarios are VS1–VS11. The maintainer ruled on
2026-09-25, approving the plan (its *Rulings this plan asks
for*, 1 to 6):
- `visitor`;
- follow-up off by default;
- narrowing by anyone for at most 12 h;
- the follow-up rule for a `confirm` answer;
- the front's credential;
- ducking and `media.announce`.

### The voice standard 0.1 (new)

Everything is new. What it holds:

| Rule | Direction | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The front listens only where the steward's voice record says, and transcribes no frame the effective listening does not admit | new | VS5, VS10; a deployed fleet's listening modes |
| Effective listening is the configured one, narrowed by narrowings and by what the front can do; never wider; reported | new | VS11; a deployed fleet's configured and effective modes |
| A narrowing applies within the narrowing delay (2 s; **looser** than the next frame first written, since a narrowing reaches the front through the steward); a front that lost the steward only narrows | new | VS5; a device cannot be reconfigured into recording what it was not started to record |
| Wake words are configuration; a word with no model falls back narrower (`wake_server` → the transcript check, `wake_device` → `tap`); declared models with their numbers | new | VS11; ruling of 2026-09-25 |
| `addressed_by` is the front's: `typed` only at an `app`, `skill` only the turn that opens a session | new | VS1, VS2, VS4 |
| Follow-up windows only after an invited, played reply, only where `follow_up` is on and listening is at least `wake_device`; a conversation ends | new | VS1, VS2; ruling 2 |
| Merging co-heard speech: overlap and agreeing text, admitted frames only, per-endpoint evidence, one id, only for a brain that declares it | new | VS3, VS10 |
| An echo, or a known machine's speech, is marked only on overlap and matching text; when unsure, a person | new | VS1, VS3, VS4; a deployed fleet's measurement that a looser text match cuts real speech |
| Every piece of speech is reported `played`: `full`, `cut` with `heard_chars`, or `dropped`; `hush` stops it | new | VS1, VS7 |
| The front speaks no words but the brain's and the installation's fixed clips; one clip when the brain is silent past the failure bound | new | VS7; a deployed fleet's fixed clips |
| The front tells the steward when it speaks | new | VS4; ruling 6 |
| The time from the end of speech to the first audio is measured and reported, not bounded | new | VS1, VS7 |
| `speaker_hint` carries its basis: `gate` only from a model enrolled on the box, `person` only at an endpoint with a person | new | VS6, VS9 |
| Audio and enrolled voices never leave the box; audio is kept only as the owner configures | new | VS5, VS6; the no-voice-profiles rule |
| The skill ingress: authenticated, one account one endpoint | new | VS4 |
| Device links admit before they read; TLS off loopback | new | A deployed fleet's admission token for LAN clients |
| A declared wake model's numbers come from the stated measurement procedure | new | The wake-word measurement procedure the voice work wrote |

### Steward 0.5

| Rule | Direction | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A section *Listening*: the endpoint gains a voice record (`listening`, `wake_words`, `zone`, `output`, `follow_up`, `heard_by`); `describe` shows it, its reported effective record and the narrowings in force (GA-LISTEN-2) | new | The design, *Endpoints and zones* |
| `narrow`, a new operation: anyone at an endpoint, any role, and its front, narrows for at most 12 h; narrowings stack; the configured record returns when the last expires (GA-LISTEN-1) | new | Ruling 3 |
| `lift_narrowing`, and every change to a voice record, are `define` change kinds, so only the owner from an endpoint not served by a brain | new | Ruling 3 |
| GA-DEF-3 gains one exception: `narrow`, which only takes listening away; it also names voice records and lifting among what a brain never changes | **looser** (`narrow`); stricter (the rest) | Ruling 3; VS5. What it opens: a guest can silence a satellite for 12 h at most |
| GA-DEF-8: a voice record whose `heard_by` names no front credential is refused | stricter | A record no front can serve |
| A new client kind, `front`: `describe`, `zone_sources`, `narrow`, `front_status`, `speaking`, nothing else (GA-AUTH-6) | new | Ruling 5, whose "may read" covers `zone_sources` |
| A notice when effective listening is narrower than configured and its narrowings, once per change of reason (GA-LISTEN-2) | new | VS11 |
| Ducking on `speaking`, through `media.duck`, with the cause `{ front, endpoint }`, as the steward's configuration | new (MAY) | Ruling 6 |
| `zone_sources`: each zone's playing `media` devices and the text they report speaking (GA-LISTEN-3) | new | VS4 |
| GA-CONF-2: the record carries `addressed_by`, `speaker_hint` and `hint_basis`, the answering endpoint's own; a record without `addressed_by` is refused; reuse keyed on `utterance_id` alone, now unique within the home | stricter | Ruling 4; VS2; one merged yes answers one plan |
| GA-CONF-6 (new): a `follow_up` answers only at an `app`, or with a `gate` hint naming the plan's speaker (or any registered person when it named none) | stricter | Ruling 4; VS2 |
| GA-TIER-3: a role `visitor` below guest, from an endpoint's `max_role` only; every step is `refuse(role)` | stricter | Ruling 1; VS10 |
| `history` keeps `narrowed` events (GA-STW-7) | new | Audit |
| A SHOULD: a notice at `define` for a name containing a wake word of an endpoint that hears it | new (SHOULD) | A deployed fleet's naming rules for voice; configurable wake words |
