# Review of the bridge standard 0.2, applier 0.8, steward 0.4 and brain 0.3

This record follows the `standard-review` procedure. The commit reviewed is named with pass 1.

## Change record

Written and committed before any reader was dispatched, and before the text itself. Directions:
**stricter**, **looser**, **moved**, **new**, **renamed**.

This revision makes the bridge standard compatible with a bridge binding a deployed fleet has run,
wherever bridge 0.1 had departed from that binding without solid ground (a scenario, a protocol
fact, or a need the fleet did not have), so that bridges written for the fleet can speak Galatea's
binding after a short list of changes. The maintainer ruled each point on 2026-09-25, weighing the
ground each departure had; the tables below cite the rulings by number, 1 to 12. The three broker facts ruling 8
rests on were checked in Mosquitto's source.

### The bridge standard 0.2

| Rule | Direction | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Topics: `{root}/bridges/{b}/status`, `lwt`, `devices`, `request/{op}`, `reply`, `event`; a device's `{root}/bridges/{b}/devices/{d}/status`, `command`, `ack`; `{root}` configured per deployment | moved | Ruling 1: the fleet's shape, per bridge, which one broker grant per bridge covers. `galatea/v1/…` goes |
| The `transports` topic goes; transports travel in the bridge's `status` | moved | Ruling 12. Planning ruling: the status already carries the bridge's view of itself |
| The major version travels as `v` in the bridge's `status`, not in the topic | moved | The topic lost `galatea/v1` |
| GA-BRIDGE-17: the bridge publishes `status` from its main loop every 10 s; the ping goes | **looser** | Ruling 2. A bridge is found dead in 30 s, not 10 s. Ground: no scenario needs 10 s (HS12 asks only that the death is noticed); a fleet ran the heartbeat |
| GA-BRIDGE-18: keepalive at most 10 s (was 6 s), a will on `lwt` carrying `instanceId`, `lwt` cleared on connect | **looser** (keepalive) | Rulings 2 and 7. Bound by `1.5 K + 10 s < 30 s` |
| GA-BRIDGE-22: a restart publishes a graceful `offline`, clears `lwt`, then disconnects cleanly | stricter | A graceful offline is distinct from a death, as the fleet's experience showed |
| GA-BRIDGE-31: `instanceId` is a UUID, new on every start; nothing about ordering survives a restart | **looser** | Ruling 7. A will whose `instanceId` is not the current status's is ignored; equality does what ordering did |
| GA-BRIDGE-13: `basisMaxAgeMs` is declared from what the bridge knows (a poll at most 3 × its cadence) or absent; the observed bound and its six constants go | **looser** | Ruling 3. The observed bound was guesswork rebuilt over four review rounds. An unknown bound still reads `stale` (fail closed), and the owner's bound wins |
| GA-BRIDGE-37: the roster in `status` carries each device's `lastCheckIn`; `status` is re-published within 1 s of a device's `observable` changing | moved | Ruling 12. The roster is the fleet's; `lastCheckIn` is Galatea's need, kept |
| GA-BRIDGE-4, GA-BRIDGE-8: one terminal `ack` per command on the device's `ack` topic: `applied` (the protocol confirmed it), `sent`, `failed` with a `reason`, or `unsupported`; the command's reply goes | moved; **looser** (no early reply) | Ruling 4. A PUBACK proves a subscriber, and the heartbeat proves the bridge. `failed` with reason `unreachable` still comes within 1 s |
| GA-BRIDGE-6: a command for a down transport, an unavailable device or an unknown device is acked `failed` within 1 s, with reason `unreachable` or `unknown_device` | moved | Was a reply; now the ack |
| GA-BRIDGE-34: the read-back follows `applied`, `sent` or `failed(no_confirmation)` | renamed | `done` is now `applied` |
| Payloads: the envelope `{commandId, issuedAt, value: {action, value}}` and ack `{commandId, source, result, reason, detail, timestamp}`; flat device status `{deviceId, state, timestamp, readings…, available, timestamps}`; camelCase and milliseconds on every bridge payload | renamed | Rulings 9 and 12 |
| GA-BRIDGE-2: a partial read's own time goes in `timestamps` for the keys it read | renamed | The fleet's device status has one `timestamp`; Galatea's need for a time per key is kept |
| MQTT 3.1.1 allowed for a bridge at Serve and Provision; the applier and Box use MQTT 5 | **looser** | Ruling 8. A 3.1.1 bridge cannot see a refused publish; that matters only where grants refuse anything, at Box. Message Expiry is enforced by the broker for a 3.1.1 subscriber (checked in Mosquitto's source) |
| GA-BRIDGE-20: a 3.1.1 bridge checks its SUBACK return codes; a refused publish it cannot see | **looser** for 3.1.1 | Ruling 8 |
| GA-BOX-1: readers other than the applier get a per-device allowlist of literal subscriptions from the owner, no wildcard; a grant on a `door_lock`, or on anything above `reversible`, is refused or warned about | stricter for Box; **looser** than 0.1's "only the applier reads" | Ruling 5. In the fleet, a content system and monitoring read device status; under Box only what the owner grants |
| `devices` required at every level | stricter than the fleet's binding | Ruling 11 |
| Below Box, a client may authenticate by password over plain TCP on a network the deployment trusts (0.1: only on one host's loopback) | **looser** | Written while drafting, for ruling 8's aim that the fleet's bridges connect unchanged; its clients authenticate by password on the site's network. At Box, unchanged |
| The bridge's `state` (`online`, `degraded`, `offline`) is derived from the roster, as in the fleet; the applier reads only `graceful` from it | new | Ruling 12. A derived `offline` (no device observable) is not the bridge's death |
| A command whose time ran out is acked `failed(expired)` (0.1: never started, and nothing said) | stricter | One terminal ack per command, as the fleet's binding has |
| An optional discard by `issuedAt` older than 120 s, acked `failed(expired)` | new | The fleet's executors do it; stricter than the ack bound, never looser |

## Pass 1

Green on `ac9fc1f`: the manifest check, the 35 unit tests, the private-reference grep and the
numbering grep. `grep -rn 'galatea/v1\|observed bound\|request/ping\|request/command\|`done`' standard/`
finds nothing outside changelog rows.

**Carried items, read before the readers.**
- The trust-centre rejoin under the device's own link key (round 7's looser move): **kept**. A
  Zigbee 3.0 device without an install code receives its unique link key under the well-known key,
  so a listener at the join learns it; that listener already holds the network key and can make a
  secured rejoin, which the text already names as its limit. The rejoin opens nothing new. The
  limit paragraph now says so (`0a13b37`).
- HS12's open-loop state and HS15's IR toggle: **deferred again**, to the revision after this one.
  Both are open design questions about open-loop devices, which this revision does not touch; it
  only makes the bridge compatible with the fleet's binding.
