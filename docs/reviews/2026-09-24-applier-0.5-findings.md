# Findings on applier standard 0.5

Findings on the committed 0.5 text, gathered on 2026-09-24 from three sources. None was fixed in
0.5. Each must be disposed of (fixed, deferred or rejected) in the review of the split (applier 0.6,
steward 0.1).

Sources:
- **S** — the scenario rewrite and its no-context review (`docs/reference/2026-09-24-home-reference-scenarios.md`, version 2);
- **R** — the stateful-layer scenario runs (a research note);
- **B** — a generic no-context review of 0.5, run as the baseline for the `standard-review` skill.

| # | Finding | Source | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Private references: a private bridge path in `related` and *What this standard governs*; a private method path in the 0.2 changelog row | S, B | blocker |
| F2 | The brain asserts `via`, `verified`, the principal and the answers to asks, and every tier refusal stands on them | S (HS16), R | blocker |
| F3 | The client asserts `principal.role`; there is no registry of persons | B | blocker |
| F4 | Delegation cannot carry `rule` or `scenario_run` principals, so a child leases a rule's action (breaks GA-LEASE-5) and re-asks confirmations authorship settled | B | blocker |
| F5 | A socket's load is not declarable; nothing bounds how long a heating load stays on | S (HS14) | blocker |
| F6 | Open-loop devices: an IR command always ends `failed(no_ack)`; state changed by a physical remote is invisible | S (HS12) | high |
| F7 | Stateless device actions (`cover.stop`, `media.duck`) always end `failed(no_ack)`; `delivered` is defined only for channels | B | high |
| F8 | A child's own rule or scenario change: `external` and leased, or relayed with its cause? The child's rule id is not namespaced (GA-META-6 against GA-META-8) | B | high |
| F9 | Nothing holds a `safety_rule` lease, so an ordinary rule can undo a safety rule's closing | B, R (latches) | high |
| F10 | A safety rule's notice is lost if whoever it is handed up to is down (GA-SAFE-4) | R | high |
| F11 | `history` keeps no `lease` or `rule_fired` events, so explanations do not survive the event window | S (HS3) | medium |
| F12 | MCP tool names contain dots (`scenario.plan`), which some LLM tool APIs reject | B | medium |
| F13 | Idempotency keys: scope and a reused key with a different body are undefined | B | medium |
| F14 | `exclusive` groups are constrained (GA-GRP-2) but never defined | B | medium |
| F15 | GA-APPLY-1's 500 ms gates a pass and is marked unmeasured; a meta-applier over an adapter likely cannot meet it | B | medium |
| F16 | No transport security required for bearer tokens | B | medium |
| F17 | A meta-applier's `revision` is not required to change when a child's does | B | medium |
| F18 | Time: no timezone in `describe`, no daylight-saving rule for `HH:MM`, no timestamp format | B | low |
| F19 | Motion sensors do not report absence; occupancy's `vacant` needs "no motion for the hold" | B | low |
| F20 | GA-TIER-2 is cited for an inline `occupancy_unknown` rule its row does not cover; GA-DESC-1, GA-TIER-1, GA-TIER-3 are never cited in the body | B | low |
| F21 | `scenario.status` has no `ended(stopped)`; an ignored start of a `single`-mode scenario has no defined result | B | low |
| F22 | GA-EVT-5 has no minimum audit period | B | low |
| F23 | "Status: candidate" in the body against `status: draft`; lowercase "must" in two sections | B | low |
| F24 | The harness checks a run id "in `describe`", but no field holds it | B | low |
| F25 | Three MUSTs cite no requirement id: consumer tolerance (marked unchecked), unknown extensions (a brain obligation), and "MUST NOT lower" a tier (GA-DESC-3's) | mechanical probe | low |
| F26 | The vocabulary has no vacuum | S (HS6) | low |

## Added by the `standard-review` skill's first runs

Two more sources, from testing the skill on the same 0.5 text:
- **P** — the skill's reader prompt (implementer, tester, attacker readings);
- **W** — the skill's scenario walk, which regraded HS1–HS16 against 0.5 at 7 as designed, 8 partly
  and 1 cannot, against the scenarios doc's own 12, 2 and 2.

| # | Finding | Source | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| F27 | `define` carries no principal or `via`; an asserted `owner` can author a rule whose `no_voice` steps count as confirmed by authorship, laundering an unlock | P, W | blocker |
| F28 | A gate or garage door on a momentary relay is `onoff`, so `reversible`: a guest can open it. The harness's own gate fixture is such a relay | W | blocker |
| F29 | A safety rule whose sensor and valve sit under different children can be hosted nowhere (GA-META-3 against GA-SAFE-6) | P | high |
| F30 | `rule_fired` and `route_conflict` are not in GA-META-8's relay, so a child's safety notice dies at the meta-applier | P | high |
| F31 | An adapter that stays up while its engine restarts leaves devices `stale`, which is no skip reason: HS7 gives `failed(no_ack)`, not a clear refusal. `stale` has no verdict anywhere | P, W | high |
| F32 | Scenario steps have no condition, so HS1's "if the room is under 19" cannot be written | W | high |
| F33 | A rule's `refused(leased)` and a safety rule's `failed` have no event, so nothing in `history`; the audit has no minimum retention (see F22) | W | high |
| F34 | `scenario.plan` gives one step per target, so a scenario acting twice on one device is mis-planned; a step skipped as `already` at plan time is never re-checked | P | high |
| F35 | The long timers (idempotency, leases, schedules, audit) need hours of real time unless the subject takes the harness's clock | P | high |
| F36 | Rooms do not merge across children: a room selector on the meta-applier misses a child's devices in the same room | W | medium |
| F37 | A safety rule's notice may say the valve closed before the valve has acked, or failed | W | medium |
| F38 | An `external` lease can be renewed without end by a device that changes by itself; a late ack after `failed(no_ack)` is read as `external` | P | medium |
| F39 | A person-started run has `person` precedence, equal to a person's manual change mid-run; whose lease a run releases is unclear for parallel runs | P | medium |
| F40 | Schedules: no rule for runs missed during downtime; a level-triggered rule's held duration across a restart is undefined (with F18) | P | medium |
| F41 | GA-APPLY-8 and GA-WIT-1 set no upper bound on how late the verdict may be reported | P | medium |
| F42 | Negative subjects cannot fail "exactly" their ids, because requirements overlap (GA-APPLY-5/9, GA-APPLY-6 with GA-SAFE-5, and others) | P | medium |
| F43 | More testable rules without ids: `scenario.stop` → `not_permitted`, "applied whole or not at all", a level rule firing once, `wait` timeouts, the delegation key (with F25) | P | medium |
| F44 | A target reached twice in one request (by id and through a group), and the order of dispatch, are undefined | P | medium |
| F45 | Whether a plan-time yes survives the re-check after a `delay` is undefined | W | medium |
| F46 | `acked` has no tolerance for quantised values (`set_level`, `set_setpoint`, `set_color`) (with F7) | P | medium |
| F47 | Terms drift from `CONTEXT.md`: `scenario_run` against `scenario`; "hold" is on *Lease*'s avoid list; *Cause* and *Group* differ | P | low |
| F48 | `verified` gates nothing | P | low |
| F49 | Scenario doc errors: HS8 names a child in the flat, which has none; HS12's planning hop overstates, and the IR blaster's transport is unstated | W | low |
| F50 | HS14: the owner can already author a maximum on-time as a safety rule and raise the tier; what is missing is the default, from a declared load (refines F5) | W | low |
