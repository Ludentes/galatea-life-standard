# Review — steward 0.12

## Why

In 0.11 a person's lease lasts a fixed `hold_time_s` from the change (7200 s by default, home-wide).
Its only traceable origin is an office energy code's cap on after-hours overrides
(the workshop's research note on manual hold time). It ignores presence: a reader in a motion-only
room loses the hold at two hours and HS3's off rule switches the light off on them, while a person
who left after five minutes keeps a light held for nearly two hours. It holds a switch-off as long
as a switch-on.

**Rulings** (the maintainer), as they bear on the text:
- 2026-10-12: "Quiet room rule seems good for now. Needs to be tested in real life." Then: put it in
  the standard now, as steward 0.12; the reference steward builds it; it is tested on the
  installation once built.
- 2026-10-12, on the brainstorm's three questions:
  - whether a schedule point ends a hold is left to the implementation and declared in `describe`;
    the reference does not end a hold at a schedule point;
  - an `external` change is held like an endpoint's, as in 0.11 (GA-LEASE-2);
  - the policy may differ by direction; the reference uses 3600 s after on, 600 s after off, and
    3600 s fixed for a device with no room or no presence sensor.
- Standing ruling, no premature policy: the safety floors stay MUSTs; the defaults and heuristics are
  the implementation's.

HS35 (a press refused under a person's lease) is the second item, by the maintainer's ruling of
2026-10-12, "Yes for physical press": a rule fired by a person's physical press acts at `person`
precedence. The six drafting decisions below were accepted by the coordinator on 2026-10-12.

## Pass 1, mechanical

Green on the draft (2026-10-12): `check_manifest.py --write`, then `check_manifest.py` clean;
`python3 -m unittest discover conformance`, 66 tests OK; the private-reference and numbered-heading
greps print nothing. Green again after round 1's fixes (84c541b5).

### Decisions to confirm

Made while drafting, not ruled:
- **The policy lives in `home.hold`**, one object both declared by `describe` and set by `define`,
  rather than a top-level `describe` field beside a separate owner setting. `describe`'s `home` is the
  policy in force, as every Home field shows its value in force; a top-level field would need a schema
  change to pass the text-map check.
- **`load_cap` leases** sit outside the quiet-room rule. Accepted by the coordinator on 2026-10-12;
  round 1 (C-M4) then gave them their own window, `load_cap_s`, so the heater's guard no longer rides
  on `fixed_s` (confirmed in round 2, K2-M3).
- **`vacant_ends` is a declared flag**, not a MUST, and a room already `vacant` at the change does not
  end the hold then, so a lamp switched on from an app in an empty room is held for its window rather
  than released at once.
- **No cap field.** A steward may not end a hold before its declared policy, so a steward wanting a
  cap needs a field the policy lacks. Left out (YAGNI, the reference has none); the gap is stated.
- **Windows of 1 s or more**, any length, are accepted from the owner; a flag may be refused only if
  the steward does not implement it.
- **A change to the policy applies at once** to the holds already held.
- **The direction of an `external` change** is the `on` it moved; one that left `on` alone is an
  other change.

### Decisions to confirm: the press

- **What counts as a press** is the author's `press: true` on an `occurrence` trigger. The applier
  declares no "person's act" meaning for an event key, so no automatic rule would be testable.
- **A `brain_authored` press rule acts at `rule` precedence**, the mark kept but inert.
- **A `{ scenario }` action of a press rule** starts its run as any rule's, at the rule's precedence;
  only the rule's own device actions are raised. A scene button that starts a scenario therefore
  does not override a person's hold.
- **The holder** is `{ rule, press: { target, key, value } }`; the applier still gets `for: { rule }`,
  so the applier's side is unchanged.
- **`confirm` steps** stay authored, as for every rule (GA-SCN-4): a guest at the switch acts with the
  author's confirmation, as before this revision.
