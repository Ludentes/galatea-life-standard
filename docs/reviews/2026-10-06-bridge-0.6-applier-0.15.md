# Review — the Zigbee bench's lessons

## Why

On 2026-10-06 the reference Zigbee bridge, installed in Docker, ran the bench network live: a new
radar presence sensor (HOBEIAN ZG-204ZV) joined, a button pusher (Tuya Fingerbot Plus) was commanded,
and a vibration sensor (Tuya TS0210) reported. Each device showed a place where the standards, or the
bridge's reading of them, were wrong or silent. The maintainer walked the eight lessons one at a
time and ruled on each, after a survey of established device vocabularies.

**Rulings** (the maintainer, 2026-10-06), as they bear on the text:
- The vocabulary's base is Home Assistant's names, formally adopted, citing a version with its date.
- `contact` is renamed `opening`, `true` meaning open; `illuminance` is added; `battery_low` is
  added, tentatively.
- A bridge maps by meaning, not by name: a limited vocabulary and a mapping layer.
- A key a definition promises is declared only once the device reports it, and that departure is
  documented heavily.
- Undescribed data is a one-time event and a list, not a standing fault.
- A setting the device confirmed survives a restart, with its confirmation time.
- Settings on any device become owner-only desired settings, delivered at the next check-in
  (tentative; the extension-actions part tentatively too).
- The freshness routes (poll an awake device, configure a report on a sleeping one, the owner's
  `fresh_s` as fallback) are covered by the scenarios; the ban on guessed bounds stays.
- Matter-style `sensing` and `hold_s` wait for the Matter bridge.
- This part is reviewed now, as applier 0.15, bridge 0.6 and steward 0.10; the applier's own
  queued items become applier 0.16.

***The maintainer's ruling on the blocker** (2026-10-06, option A of three): a bridge may hold
back only a key its maker's records name, for that model, as promised and seen missing, and only a
sensor key or an extension `state` key. Every other key is declared from the start, so `leak` is never
held back. The other two options were: hold back any key except safety-relevant ones (the line is
fuzzy), and declare everything and flag never-reported keys (a rule on a never-sent reading accepted
with a warning). With it: `devices` before the first value's status; a reported key kept across
restarts and never held back again; the applier relays `undescribed`; GA-DEF-12 counts a
`session.<token>` key as declared while its token is in `accounts`, and says it checks at `define`
only.

**Direction changes since round 1's record:** GA-BRIDGE-80 moved **stricter** (from any key to named keys
of two kinds); GA-BRIDGE-79 **stricter** (dropped on rejoin); GA-BRIDGE-78 **looser** (a movement `false`
may be published, meaning nothing: Claude H2, walk F1, HS3); GA-BRIDGE-5 **looser** (excepts a kept,
confirmed setting: Kimi H1). New negative subjects `holds-back-the-leak`, `declares-after-the-reading`,
`publishes-an-unconfirmed-setting`. Round 2's readers see this record.
