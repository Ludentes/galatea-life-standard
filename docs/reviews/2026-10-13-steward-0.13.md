# Review — steward 0.13

## Why

First light's FL-GAP-6 ([`docs/specs/2026-10-11-first-light-acceptance.md`](https://github.com/Ludentes/galatea-life/blob/main/docs/specs/2026-10-11-first-light-acceptance.md)): a plug back from a power
cut reported five settings it had never reported, each a first value with cause `external`, and each
took a person's hold on the plug for an hour (GA-LEASE-2). The owner's rule `bench-switch-off` was
`refused(leased)` all that hour. FL-GAP-6 itself is the applier's to fix; it showed that the owner has
no way to let their own work through a person's hold, apart from GA-LEASE-10's guest pass.

**Rulings** (the maintainer), as they bear on the text:
- 2026-10-13: the owner can override persons' holds at three scopes, per rule, per device and
  home-wide (the backlog's *Steward 0.13, as ruled*).
- 2026-10-13, the floor, agreed the same day: no scope passes a `safety_rule` lease, a `load_cap`
  lease or a run's lease, and work a brain authored (`brain_authored` for the device) never passes.
- The steward session's rulings on the shape: one mechanism, GA-LEASE-10's, generalised to every
  person's hold, with three switches named `passes_holds` (a rule), `owner_work_passes_holds` (a
  target) and `owner_work_passes_holds` (the home's `hold`); every steward implements all three; no
  per-schedule switch.
- Scope: this change only. The other items in the backlog's *Steward 0.12, what the build leaves for
  0.13* stay there.

### Decisions to confirm

- **`passes_holds` on a `brain_authored` rule is accepted and has no effect** while the mark stands.
  Cost if wrong: an owner who redefines a brain's rule whole, to raise its tier, also switches on a
  pass the brain set; `describe` and the `confirm_define` diff show the field. *Round 1 (K1, H1):
  reversed; through a brain a rule's `passes_holds` is never set or cleared.*
- **Through a brain, a target's `owner_work_passes_holds` is never changed** (GA-DEF-3). Not in the
  design; added because the device's switch is a policy over persons' holds, as the home's is.
  *Round 1: kept, with the stage and atomicity stated (K7) and a switched target's delete refused
  (L1).*
- **A passing step names nothing new**: no field on the plan step or outcome, no `lease` event.
  Cost if wrong: `history` alone does not say why a rule acted on a held device; a reader needs
  `describe`. *Round 1 (F1): reversed; the step records `passed_hold: { holder, switch }`.*
- **The reference's `owner_work_passes_holds` is `false`.** The standard keeps the home's value the
  steward's own, as every hold field.
- **A switch's change applies at once**, to holds already held, judged at a step's planning and
  dispatch, as a hold policy change does (GA-LEASE-8).
